Parts of speechnoun=ชื่อ · verb=การกระทำ · adj.=ขยายนามThe brilliant stars (adj.)
Simple pastV-ed / รูปอดีตไม่ปกติwake→woke, go→went, fall→fell, take→took, see→saw, come→came, eat→ate
Past continuouswas/were + V-ing (while = เหตุการณ์ยาว)While he was cutting the tree, his ax slipped.
Simple futurewill + V1I will help you.
